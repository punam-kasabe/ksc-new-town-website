About KSC New Town

Karnala-Sai-Chirner (KSC) New Town, also known as "Third Mumbai", is a massive planned urban development in Maharashtra’s Raigad district. The project spans approximately 323.44 square kilometres and aims to reduce congestion in Mumbai and Navi Mumbai while developing a new global economic hub.

The city is being developed under the MMRDA (Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority) which acts as the New Town Development Authority. The project covers 124 villages across Uran, Panvel and Pen tehsils in the Raigad region.

KSC New Town – Third Mumbai

KSC New Town (Karnala-Sai-Chirner New Town) is one of the largest planned smart city developments in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region. It is strategically located near major infrastructure projects like the Atal Setu (Mumbai Trans Harbour Link) and the upcoming Navi Mumbai International Airport.

Key Project Details

  • Official Name: Karnala-Sai-Chirner (KSC) New Town
  • Authority: MMRDA (Mumbai Metropolitan Region Development Authority)
  • Area: 323.44 Sq Km Mega City
  • Coverage: 124 villages across Uran, Panvel & Pen
  • Connectivity: Atal Setu & Navi Mumbai International Airport

Core Vision & Features

  • Smart city infrastructure & sustainable planning
  • Edu City with global universities
  • Medi City for healthcare & biotechnology
  • Commercial & IT hubs similar to Bandra-Kurla Complex
  • Modern residential townships & green landscapes

Current Development Status

The project is currently in its planning and infrastructure phase. Drone mapping, LiDAR surveys and GIS-based master planning are underway to finalize the development blueprint. Major international investors including Brookfield and Blackstone have shown strong interest with investment proposals worth approximately ₹3.5 lakh crore.
